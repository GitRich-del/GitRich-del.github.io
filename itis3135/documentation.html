Maintenance

Introduction

Maintaining your Resilient Shark gear is crucial for ensuring its durability and style. This documentation covers the best practices for each item.

Gear_Status = "Resilient"

Cleaning Your Beanie

The Resilient Beanie should be hand-washed to preserve the shark-strength fibers.

  • Use cold water
  • Mild detergent only
  • Air dry away from direct sunlight

Polishing the Chain

Keep your Silver Shark Chain shining by polishing it weekly.

Chain.shine()

Pendant Storage

Always store your RS Pendant in the provided micro-fiber pouch to prevent scratches and maintain its resilient luster.

  • Verify pouch is dry
  • Avoid contact with other metals

Wristband Care

The ITIS3135 wristbands are designed for salt-water resistance but should be rinsed with fresh water after use to prevent salt buildup.

Wristband.rinse(water_type="fresh")

Firmware Updates

For smart gear components, ensure your firmware is updated to the latest Resilient Shark OS version.

rs-cli update --gear all
